# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming and productive space for everyone.
Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted contact;
- respect project maintainers' decisions and the project's scope.

Reports can be made privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.
Reports will be reviewed in confidence where practical. Maintainers may
remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when conduct is
inconsistent with this policy.
